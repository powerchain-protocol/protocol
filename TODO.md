# Vision

PowerChain is building the programmable infrastructure for the global sustainable economy.

This roadmap represents the evolution of the ecosystem—from a high-performance blockchain powered by the **PowerChain Virtual Machine (PVM)** to a comprehensive platform for renewable energy, enterprise finance, artificial intelligence, environmental markets and tokenised real-world assets.

**Build Sustainable Infrastructure. Build on PowerChain.**
