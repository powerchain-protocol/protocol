# Vision

PowerChain Governance enables the community, validators, developers, enterprises and ecosystem participants to collectively guide the evolution of the network.

Through transparent proposal processes, decentralised voting, responsible treasury management and secure protocol upgrades, governance ensures that the PowerChain ecosystem remains open, resilient and aligned with its mission to build the digital infrastructure of the global sustainable economy.
