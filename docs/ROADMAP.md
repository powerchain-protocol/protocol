# PowerChain Roadmap

This roadmap describes direction rather than a contractual delivery commitment. Status is verified against repository code, tests, deployments, and release notes.

## Foundation — current beta

- Monorepo build and install policy
- Web, dashboard, backend, schemas, SDKs, and program workspaces
- Renewable marketplace, merchant workflows, RWA portfolio, energy IoT, and certificates
- Solana/Anchor, native SVM/Pinocchio, and Sui foundations
- Authentication, roles, SaaS workspace, API, and documentation foundations

## Testnet readiness

- Reproducible builds and locked toolchains
- Real program IDs and deployment manifests
- End-to-end tests and local validators
- Oracle evidence prototype and smart-meter provenance
- Explorer, faucet, SDK, CLI, telemetry, and incident runbooks

## Protocol expansion

- Proof of Generation specification
- Energy and carbon registries
- Certificate issuance/retirement
- Governance and treasury programs
- Cross-chain messaging and policy controls

## Production readiness

- Independent security audits
- Operational SLOs and disaster recovery
- Validator onboarding and upgrade process
- Compliance and jurisdictional review
- Governance activation and transparent treasury reporting
