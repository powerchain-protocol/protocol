# Renewable Energy Marketplace

PowerChain's merchant dashboard now groups local renewable discovery, peer-to-peer trading, maps, Wayfinder optimization, donations and crowdfunding.

## Routes

- `/dashboard/merchant/marketplace` — searchable local energy offers
- `/dashboard/merchant/map` — local generation and flexibility map
- `/dashboard/merchant/wayfinder` — demand-to-supply route optimizer
- `/dashboard/merchant/energy` — P2P energy exchange
- `/dashboard/merchant/donate` — public-benefit donations
- `/dashboard/merchant/crowdfunding` — renewable project funding

## Safety and settlement

The included UI and APIs create quotes and intents. Production deployments must verify meter signatures, asset ownership, jurisdictional energy-market rules, contributor eligibility and wallet authorization before settlement.
